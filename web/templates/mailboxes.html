Before you go live

  • SPF, DKIM and DMARC: three DNS records that prove your emails are really from you. Google Workspace sets SPF and DKIM up in Admin console under Apps, Google Workspace, Gmail, Authenticate email. Your domain host's help pages cover the rest.
  • Test it: send one email to mail-tester.com and aim for 9/10 or better.
