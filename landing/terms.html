Last updated: August 16, 2026

1. Who we are

Replyworthy ("we", "us") is an outreach service that finds leads, writes them personal emails and sorts the replies for you. You can reach us any time at itsshanco@gmail.com. By creating an account or using the service you agree to these terms. If you do not agree with them, please do not use Replyworthy.

2. What Replyworthy does

Replyworthy finds businesses and people that match what you sell, researches them, writes each one a personal email, sends it from an inbox you connect, follows up politely, and reads and sorts every answer. When someone is interested, a person (you) takes over. The service never writes back to a prospect on its own.

3. Your account

  • Give us accurate information when you sign up, and keep it current.
  • Keep your password to yourself. Anything done from your account is treated as done by you.
  • The free trial is meant to be used once per person or business. Creating extra accounts to collect more free credits is not allowed.

4. Plans and credits

Usage is measured in lead credits. Each plan grants a set number of credits every month. Plans also differ in a small set of published limits, exactly as shown on the billing page in your account: how many offer profiles and active campaigns you can run at once, whether person leads are available, and how long unused credits roll over.

  • One credit is one business lead: found, researched, scored and personally written to. One lead for a named person at a company costs two credits, because verified contact data costs us more to buy. Person leads are available on the Growth and Scale plans.
  • You are only charged for leads we actually deliver to you. Leads we filter out or skip cost you nothing.
  • Unused credits roll over for one month, up to the size of one monthly grant (two months and two grants on the Scale plan). Older credits expire.
  • Plans also come with service guards (for example how many searches can run at once) to keep the service fast and sending safe for everyone. These guards are the same on every paid plan and are not sold as features.
  • How fast you can send is set by your own inboxes, not by your plan. New inboxes are ramped up gradually to protect their reputation, and we do not offer or promise unlimited daily sending.
  • We may change plans or pricing over time. If a change affects you, we will tell you by email before it does.

5. Billing and payments

Payments are handled by Paddle, our merchant of record. That means Paddle is the seller on your receipt: they process your card, handle sales taxes and keep your payment details safe. We never see your card number.

  • Paid plans renew monthly until you cancel.
  • You can change your card or cancel any time from the billing portal linked in your account.
  • If a payment fails, your workspace is paused: you can still log in and read everything, but searching and sending stop until the payment goes through. Nothing is deleted.

6. Refunds

Our refund policy is short and lives on its own page: the refund policy. In one line: the free trial is how you try Replyworthy, and payments already made are not refunded except where the law requires it.

7. Acceptable use

Replyworthy sends email to real people, so this section matters most. By using the service you agree to all of the following:

  • Follow the email laws that apply to you and to the people you contact. In the United States that includes the CAN-SPAM Act.
  • Be honest about who you are. Your sender name, postal address and reply address must be real.
  • No misleading subject lines, no illegal offers, no adult content, nothing hateful or harassing.
  • Every email includes an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribes are honored instantly. Working around this, or contacting someone who opted out, will get your account closed.
  • Do not import bought spam lists. Replyworthy finds and researches leads for you; that is the whole point.

The service protects itself and its users automatically: if bounces climb or sending looks harmful, sending pauses on its own. We may also pause or close any account that breaks these rules, without a refund, because one bad sender hurts everyone else.

8. Your data is yours

The leads you find, the campaigns you run and the replies you get belong to you. You can export them whenever you like. We do not sell your data and we do not reuse your leads for anyone else. The details of what we store and why are in the privacy policy.

9. What we do not promise

Replyworthy is provided as it is. We work hard to keep it accurate and reliable, but we cannot promise that every search finds leads, that every email lands in an inbox, or that anyone replies. Results depend on your offer, your market and the reputation of the inbox you send from.

10. Our liability

If something goes wrong and we are found liable to you, our total liability is capped at the amount you paid us in the twelve months before the issue. We are not liable for indirect losses such as lost profits or lost business opportunities.

11. Cancelling and closing your account

  • Cancel any time from the billing portal. Your plan, credits and access stay active until the end of the period you already paid for.
  • After cancellation your workspace stays readable for about 30 days so you can export your data. After that it is archived.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and data entirely by emailing us.

12. Changes, law and contact

If we make a meaningful change to these terms we will email you before it takes effect. These terms are governed by the laws of India. Questions are welcome at itsshanco@gmail.com.
