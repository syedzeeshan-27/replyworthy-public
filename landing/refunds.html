Refund Policy

Last updated: July 11, 2026

The short version: try Replyworthy free before you pay a rupee or a dollar. Once a month is paid for, it is not refunded, but you can cancel anytime and nothing you paid for is taken away early.

Try before you pay

Every new account starts with 25 free lead credits and needs no card. That trial exists precisely so you can watch Replyworthy find leads and write emails for your market before any money changes hands. Please use it.

No refunds on payments made

Plans are month to month. Amounts already charged are not refunded, in full or in part, except where the law of your country gives you a right to one. There are no refunds for unused credits or for months where you did not log in.

Cancel anytime

You can cancel from the billing portal in your account, in a couple of clicks, with no one to convince. Your plan, your credits and your access all stay active until the end of the month you already paid for. After that, no further charges.

If a payment fails

Your workspace pauses instead of disappearing. You can still log in and read everything; searching and sending resume as soon as the payment goes through. Nothing is deleted.

How refunds work when the law requires one

Payments are processed by Paddle, our merchant of record, and any refund the law requires is issued through them back to your original payment method. If you think this applies to you, email itsshanco@gmail.com and a person will sort it out with you.
