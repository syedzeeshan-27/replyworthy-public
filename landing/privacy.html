Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 11, 2026

1. What we collect and why

  • Your account: your email address and password. The password is stored hashed, which means even we cannot read it.
  • Your workspace: the offers you describe, the searches you run, your campaigns, and the leads and replies they produce. That is the product doing its job.
  • Connected inboxes: when you connect an inbox, its credentials are stored encrypted and used only to send your emails and read your replies. They are never written to logs and never shown to anyone.
  • Payment status: Paddle, our payment provider, tells us whether your subscription is active. Your card number stays with Paddle. We never see it.
  • One cookie: a single session cookie that keeps you logged in. No advertising trackers, on this site or in the app.
  • Email you send us: if you write to support, we keep the conversation so we can help you.

2. Data about the people you contact

To find leads for you, Replyworthy reads publicly available business information such as websites and public listings, and licensed business contact data from professional data providers. This is processed on your behalf so the service can write relevant, personal emails.

Everyone contacted through Replyworthy gets a working unsubscribe link. When someone opts out, that choice takes effect immediately and is kept on a suppression list so they are not contacted again.

3. Services we rely on

Replyworthy runs on a small set of well known services, each doing one job:

  • Render hosts the application and its database.
  • Anthropic provides the AI that researches leads, writes drafts and sorts replies.
  • Professional data providers supply business and contact information for lead searches.
  • Paddle handles payments, as merchant of record.
  • Your own email provider (for example Google) does the actual sending, because emails go out from the inbox you connect.

Each of these receives only what it needs to do its job, nothing more.

4. What we never do

  • We do not sell personal data. Not yours, not your leads'.
  • We do not reuse your leads or your campaigns for other customers.
  • We do not show ads or place advertising trackers.

5. Keeping and deleting data

Your data stays as long as your account does. If you cancel, your workspace stays readable for about 30 days so you can export everything, then it is archived. Email us at any time to have your account and its data deleted, or to get a copy of what we hold about you.

6. Security

All traffic runs over HTTPS. Inbox credentials are stored encrypted, passwords are stored hashed, and access to production systems is limited to the people who run the service.

7. Questions and changes

If we make a meaningful change to this policy we will email you before it takes effect. For any question, request or complaint about your data, write to itsshanco@gmail.com and a person will answer.
