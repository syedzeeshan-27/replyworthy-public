How it works

Four steps. None of them yours.

A lead enters on the left and comes out as a warm conversation on your calendar. You do not touch a single step in between.

1

Find

It reads local businesses' own websites and picks the ones that actually need what you sell. No stale databases, no scraped junk lists.

2

Write

Every business gets its own email, written from what is on their site. Never a template, never a blast. Sent only during their business hours.

3

Understand

It reads every reply and knows the difference between “how much”, “not now”, and “I’m on holiday”. It nudges, waits, or hands over.

4

Hand over

Interested? Your phone buzzes and a real person picks up the thread from here. No prospect ever talks to a bot.
